Looking across more than a century.

The 1907 plan offers an intriguing way to look across the old township and its surrounding country. Placing the historic lines over modern mapping helps reveal how the proposed town layout related to the landscape.

The historic map was positioned using the Magnet mill site and the eastern school reserve as reference points. Its alignment is provisional and intended for town-scale exploration, not cadastral or property-boundary decisions.
