The Gaunt name at Magnet.

These are only some of the references found under one name. Together they show the texture of everyday Magnet life.

Magnet Tennis Club

On the court

Norman Gaunt and Essie Gaunt were identified in an undated club photograph. H. S. Gaunt is also recorded as a tennis organiser and player between 1912 and 1922.

St Patrick’s Church

Music and community

Mrs N. Gaunt is recorded as organist and choir director. She also helped with the church bazaar.

Magnet State School · 1921

Helping at school

Mrs W. A. Gaunt was named among those who gave valuable assistance at the school tree planting.

Town directories

A familiar name in town.

Across the Tasmanian directories, Norman Gaunt appears as an accountant at Magnet from 1910 to 1924. Small entries like these help build a picture of the people and work that shaped the town.
