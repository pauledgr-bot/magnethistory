Research source 01

Archives &
public records.

Official records provide the durable framework for Magnet’s history: they help establish names, dates, places and the institutions that shaped the community.

The paper trail

The official record of Magnet.

These sources are used alongside newspapers, photographs and family knowledge. They provide leads that can be compared, checked and placed in context as the history of Magnet is reconstructed.

01

Mining Department records

Mining records help trace the field’s development, the activity of mines and the official administration of the district.

02

Government Gazettes & parliamentary papers

Government publications can record appointments, notices, decisions and matters affecting the mining communities of Tasmania’s north-west.

03

Births, deaths, marriages & electoral rolls

Family-history records help identify people, household connections and the changing population associated with Magnet.

04

Land titles, valuation & rate records

Property and local records can help place people and businesses within the physical settlement and its surrounding district.

05

Post office, school, church & local-government records

Institutional records add detail about the services and organisations that supported everyday life in and around Magnet.

Research in progress

Records are evidence, not the whole story.
